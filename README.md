### 🔬 HCT-CBAM — Hybrid CNN-Transformer
[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/IEEE_SUBMISSION-00629B?style=flat-square&logo=ieee&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/lokeshtheprogrammer/cnnvit)
[![Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/CODE-0d1117?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/lokeshtheprogrammer/cnnvit)

Fine-grained food classification. **81.4% Top-1 · 95.2% Top-5**
on Food-101 across 5 seeds at 31.2M params — **+18.3pp** over
ResNet50, **+4.6pp** over CMT-S. Pre-tokenization CBAM yields a
**1.87× transformer amplification** effect.

`PyTorch` `ViT` `CBAM` `Food-101`

</td>

`Python` `Drain3` `SSE` `Docker`
